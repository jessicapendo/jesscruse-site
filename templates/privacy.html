Privacy

This page explains, in plain words, what this site does with your information.

When you pre-order the book

The form asks for your name, email, city, country, the format you want and how many copies. I use these only to keep my pre-order list, to send you a thank-you, and to tell you when the book is ready. I do not sell or share your details. You pay nothing when you pre-order.

Your details come to my own email and Google account, through a form service that only passes them on to me. If you want me to remove you from the list, email me and I will.

Counting visits

I count how many times pages are viewed and which buttons are clicked, so I can see what is useful. This uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and does not record who you are.

Other services

Some buttons take you to other services, such as Calendly for booking a call, Substack for my writing, YouTube and Spotify for interviews, and Voxa for the app. Each has its own privacy policy, which applies once you are there. Videos on this site load from YouTube's privacy-friendly player.

If you email me, I use your details only to reply to you.
